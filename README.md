# Social Service Financing in Czechia
Analysis of social services funding in Czechia using Python and Tableau. Includes data cleaning, standardization, and quality checks, with an **interactive dashboard** exploring funding by region, service type, and funding source over time.

## Project objective

The project explores how social services are funded across regions,
service types, and funding sources over time.

## What this repository contains

- `socfin_23102026.ipynb` — Python notebook containing the analysis.
- `README.md` — project introduction and documentation.

## Analytical workflow

The project focuses on:
- Cleaning and standardizing data from different sources.
- Checking data quality and consistency.
- Analyzing social service funding.
- Presenting results through Tableau visualizations.

## Data sources
The project combines the following datasets on public funding
for social services in the Czech Republic:

- Grant allocations for social services with supra-regional
  or nationwide coverage.
  Publisher: Ministry of Labour and Social Affairs (MPSV).
  Source: [MPSV — Social services grants](https://mpsv.gov.cz/dotace-na-poskytovani-socialnich-sluzeb).

- Grant allocations for social services within regional networks.
  Publishers: Regional authorities and the City of Prague.
  Sources: The respective regional authorities' websites.

- Individual grants awarded by MPSV.
  Publisher: Ministry of Labour and Social Affairs (MPSV).
  Source: [MPSV — Financing of social services](https://mpsv.gov.cz/financovani-socialnich-sluzeb).

- Individual grants awarded by regional authorities.
  Publishers: Regional authorities and the City of Prague.
  Sources: The respective regional authorities' websites.

- Municipal expenditure on social services.
  Publisher: Ministry of Finance — MONITOR State Treasury portal.
  Source: [MONITOR — Open data catalogue](https://monitor.statnipokladna.gov.cz/datovy-katalog/open-data).

Dataset names are descriptive English labels.
Links to individual regional source documents will be added.
